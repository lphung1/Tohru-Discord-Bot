# AI Automation Consulting — Pricing Structure

## Business Model

Managed automation as a service. You own, build, and operate the workflows.
Clients pay for outcomes, not software seats. Retainer-based with setup fees.

---

## Core Pricing Philosophy

- Price on **business value delivered**, not technical features
- Retainer model creates recurring revenue and long-term client relationships
- Setup fee covers build cost, monthly retainer covers ongoing management
- Never expose infrastructure costs or "executions" to clients — abstract it
- Upsell by adding services to existing retainer, not by jumping tiers

## Market-Specific Pricing Notes

### Law Firms
- Premium due to compliance requirements and risk sensitivity
- Minimum: Growth tier ($997) — compliance tier pushes to Enterprise
- Emphasize: client intake automation, document routing, deadline reminders
- Lead with ROI: one paralegal hour saved per day = $3,000-5,000/mo saved

### Healthcare (Clinics, Dental, Chiro)
- Strong ROI on appointment reminders (reduce no-shows 20-40%)
- HIPAA requirement pushes toward Enterprise tier
- Emphasize: appointment booking, recall campaigns, intake forms
- Add-on: insurance verification workflow

### Real Estate Agencies
- High lead volume, strong fit for email sequences + CRM automation
- Price-sensitive — Growth tier sweet spot
- Emphasize: lead follow-up speed (5-minute response vs 48-hour average)
- Add-on: listing update notifications, buyer/seller drip campaigns

### Home Services (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical)
- Strong fit for AI call answering (calls during jobs)
- Starter or Growth tier
- Emphasize: never miss a call, after-hours booking
- Add-on: review request automation post-job

### Restaurants / Hospitality
- Budget-sensitive, Starter tier only unless multi-location
- Emphasize: reservation confirmation, review management, staff scheduling notifications
- Multi-location: Growth or Professional

### E-commerce / Retail
- Strong fit for email automation, abandoned cart, post-purchase sequences
- Growth tier typical
- Emphasize: revenue recovery from abandoned carts (avg 15-20% recovery rate)

## Pricing Psychology and Sales Notes

**Anchor high, then present options:**
Present Professional first, then Growth as "most popular," then Starter as entry.
Clients anchor to Professional pricing — Growth feels like a deal.

**Setup fee justification:**
Frame as "we're investing in your business." Setup covers discovery, build, testing, onboarding. Never waive fully — discount max 50% on setup to close.

**Monthly vs annual:**
Offer 2 months free on annual commitment.
Annual: Starter $4,970, Growth $9,970, Professional $19,970
Improves cash flow and reduces churn.

**ROI framing by service:**

| Service | Typical SMB ROI claim |
|---|---|
| Email automation | 3-5hrs/wk staff time saved |
| AI call answering | 0% missed calls after hours |
| Website chat | 30-50% more leads captured overnight |
| Client response automation | Response time from 24hrs to 5min |
| Internal workflow automation | 5-10hrs/wk admin time eliminated |

**Discovery call → proposal process:**
1. 30min discovery: understand their current manual processes
2. Identify 1-2 highest ROI quick wins
3. Propose Growth or Professional with specific workflow list
4. Show 3 comparable use cases or testimonials
5. Setup fee + first month on signing
